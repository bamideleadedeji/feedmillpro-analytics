FeedMillPro Analytics: Commercial Feed Milling Enterprise Suite 🏭FeedMillPro Analytics is a specialized Python-based SaaS application built with Streamlit for feedmill operators, toll millers, nutritionist consultants, and commercial feed manufacturers. It streamlines raw material formulation budgeting, processes shrinkage/moisture losses, tracks energy overhead, and generates automated batch audit reports across multiple livestock feed types.🌟 Key FeaturesMulti-Formulation Profiles: Preset inclusion templates and customizable parameters for Broiler Starter, Broiler Finisher, Layer Mash, Grower Mash, and Aquafeed / Fish Feed.Least-Cost Formulation Pricing: Computes batch raw material costs based on volatile market prices for Maize, Soya Meal, Wheat Offal, Premixes, Limestone, and Bone Meal.Shrinkage & Operational Overhead: Accounts for moisture evaporation, dust, and spillage losses ($0.5\% - 5.0\%$) alongside generator diesel, milling labor, and bag packaging costs.Grain Price Stress-Testing: Interactive sensitivity matrices modeling net profit variations under dual grain price shocks (Maize vs. Soya price shifts).Automated Audit Exports: Instant download buttons for CSV budget sheets and in-memory PDF executive feasibility reports.🛠️ Project StructurePlaintext├── app.py              # Core Streamlit application & interactive UI
